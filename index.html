The problem isn't the draft

AI didn't take your voice. It took the last 20%.

Here's what actually happened in the last eighteen months.

Writing got faster than editing. So naturally, everybody stopped editing. Which means the entire internet now speaks in the same three rhythms: the teeter-totter ("it's not just a tool, it's a philosophy"), the three-beat drum (fast, clean, and reliable), and the hedge fog (may potentially help in some cases).

Your reader has seen this shape ten thousand times. They don't need to run it through a detector. They feel it in the first line — and they leave.

The tells are structural, not cosmetic:

  • No evidence. Plausible sentences that say nothing verifiable
  • No rhythm. Every sentence 14–18 words — the "even hum"
  • No position. Balanced on every question, committed to none

Swap a few words and you'll still have all three problems. That's why "make it sound more human" never works — it's working on the wrong layer.

The mechanism

E·S·D — fix the right layer, in the right order.

Every false-sounding text fails on one of three layers. ProofMark® addresses them in sequence — because polishing rhythm on text that has nothing to say is wasted work.

LAYER 1

E — Evidence

Add 3 verifiable specifics per section. Numbers, names, dates, real actions. Turns opinion into information.

LAYER 2

S — Structure

Break the even hum on purpose. Short landings, long builds, uneven lists. This is what a human ear recognises.

LAYER 3

D — Distinction

Stake one position you'd defend in an argument. Nothing signals authorship like being willing to be wrong.

Run E·S·D and the "is this AI?" question stops being interesting. The reader has a reason to keep reading — and by the time they quote your line back to you, it doesn't matter how the draft started. It matters who signed it.

The system

Four moves. Seventy-five minutes. Repeatable forever.

Move 1 · 10 min

BRIEF

Nine questions before you prompt. Who's reading, what they believe, the one number only you'd know. Most people skip this and then blame the model — the model had no target. You were the missing ingredient.

Move 2 · 10 min

LAYERS

Never ask for the finished document. Skeleton → flesh → evidence → provenance. One bounded section at a time, or the model starts averaging toward the middle of everything it's ever read.

Move 3 · 45 min

BRAND

The Five Passes: Fog (strip the tells) → Cadence (break the hum) → Specific (add proof) → Stake (take a position) → Receipt (show you were there). One pass, one job. Mark first, fix later.

Move 4 · 60 sec

MARK

The ProofMark Card — seven checks before anything leaves your hands. Read aloud. Any 28-word sentence. Any triple. Any claim without a number. Any opening that could belong to another topic. Then ship.
